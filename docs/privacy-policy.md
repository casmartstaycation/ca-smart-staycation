# CA Smart Staycation Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 11, 2026**

CA Smart Staycation respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the CA Smart Staycation website and Android application.

**Business:** CA Smart Staycation  
**Privacy contact:** booking@casmartstaycation.com  
**Website:** https://casmartstaycation.com

## Information you provide

When making a booking or contacting CA Smart Staycation, you may provide information such as your name, contact details, stay dates, number of guests, booking preferences, payment information or proof of payment, and other information necessary to process your reservation.

## How information is used

Information submitted through the service may be used to:

- Process and manage reservations
- Communicate with guests about bookings
- Verify payments when applicable
- Provide accommodation and stay-related services
- Respond to customer support requests
- Maintain security and prevent misuse
- Operate, maintain, and improve the booking service

## Photos and uploaded files

If the booking service permits file uploads, files are processed for the purpose for which they are submitted, such as payment verification or booking administration. Do not upload information that is not required for your reservation.

## Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the booking website, application, hosting, database, file storage, communications, and other infrastructure. Such providers should only receive information necessary to provide their services.

CA Smart Staycation does not sell personal information to advertisers.

## Data retention

Booking and related information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to manage reservations, accounting, customer service, legal obligations, security, and legitimate business records. Retention periods may vary by type of information.

## Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## Your choices and requests

For privacy questions, correction requests, or deletion requests where applicable, contact **booking@casmartstaycation.com**. Some information may need to be retained when required for legitimate business, legal, accounting, or security purposes.

## Children's privacy

The service is intended for general booking use and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children without appropriate adult involvement.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the service, technology, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at the public privacy-policy location used for the service.
