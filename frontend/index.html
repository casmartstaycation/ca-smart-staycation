Booking Information

💰 Security Deposit

A ₱1,000 Security Deposit is required for accommodation bookings. It is refundable within 1–2 days after checkout, subject to inspection of the accommodation.

👥 Additional Guests

Your accommodation includes 2 guests. Every additional guest is charged ₱300 per night.

👶 Children

Children aged 0–2 years old stay FREE. They are NOT counted toward the maximum guest capacity.

🏡 Maximum Capacity

Maximum accommodation capacity is 4 guests.

Vehicle information is required for guests who selected Parking Only or Accommodation + Parking. These details will be used by CA Smart Staycation to verify vehicle ownership, register your vehicle for parking access, and enhance the security of all guests and their vehicles during their stay.

Please ensure that the information provided is accurate. The registered vehicle details must match the vehicle that will enter the property.
